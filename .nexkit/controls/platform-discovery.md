# Axis read-only discovery

Investigate only the approved issue specification and its acceptance criteria.
Read AGENTS.md and docs/product-brief.md first. Axis is a new enterprise
application platform; architecture, stack and the first implementation scope
are open decisions.

Inspect actual repository files and configured behavior. Cite exact files or
external evidence for findings. Clearly distinguish observations, proposals and
unresolved decisions. Do not treat infrastructure checks as application tests.
Do not change source, configuration, workflows or GitHub settings, and do not
manufacture human approval. Return a concise report addressing the approved
questions, constraints, evidence, limitations and decisions needed from the owner.

Keep Axis requirements and reports self-contained. Do not name, link or require
unrelated projects or their source paths. Use general knowledge to explain
Axis's own needs without introducing cross-project dependencies.
