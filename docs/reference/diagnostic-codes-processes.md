# Process diagnostic codes

This page lists the codes that processes report: their steps, blocks and variables, and the triggers, events, endpoints, connectors, webhooks and notifications that they use. The shape of a diagnostic and the numbering rule are in [diagnostic codes](diagnostic-codes.md).

| Code | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `AXC0067` | A process's `entity` names no loaded entity. Reported at `/entity`. Not reported when the name is an entity file that was not loaded because of its own errors. |
| `AXC0068` | A process's `entity` is a child entity. Reported at `/entity`, naming the owner. |
| `AXC0069` | An earlier step of the same process already uses this `name`, ignoring letter case. Reported at `/steps/{i}/name` of the later step, naming the first one. The later step is left out of `AXC0071`, `AXC0072` and `AXC0073`. |
| `AXC0070` | A process's `start`, a branch `next`, an `otherwise`, an operation's `next`, a task outcome's `next`, a task deadline's `next` or a `waitEvent` timeout's `next` names no step of the process, ignoring letter case. Reported at that property, such as `/start`, `/steps/{i}/branches/{j}/next`, `/steps/{i}/next`, `/steps/{i}/outcomes/{j}/next`, `/steps/{i}/deadline/next` or `/steps/{i}/timeout/next`. |
| `AXC0071` | A process step cannot be reached from `start`. Reported at `/steps/{i}`. Not checked when `start` names no step. |
| `AXC0072` | A process step has no path to an `end` step. Reported at `/steps/{i}`. Not reported for a step with a transition to an unknown step, which is already `AXC0070`. |
| `AXC0073` | Process steps form a cycle with no waiting step, such as a → b → a of `decision` and `operation` steps, which would run for ever. A cycle through a `task`, a `timer` or a `waitEvent` step is allowed, because the transitions of such a step are not followed. Reported at the transition that closes the cycle, walking the steps in file order and each step's transitions in order: the branches, then `otherwise`, then `next`, then the outcomes, then the deadline's `next`, then the timeout's `next`. The message names the steps in the cycle. See [limits](processes.md#limits) and [Loops](process-flow.md#loops). |
| `AXC0078` | A process operation step's `operation` is not `updateRecord`, `callConnector`, `raiseEvent`, `createRecord`, `notify` or a package operation written `<package>/<operation>`, matched exactly. Reported at `/steps/{i}/operation`. Its other properties are then not checked. |
| `AXC0079` | A key of an `updateRecord` `set` names no field of the subject entity, or a key of a `createRecord` `set` names no field of its `entity`, or a field an earlier key of the step already sets, ignoring letter case. Reported at `/steps/{i}/set/{field}`. |
| `AXC0080` | A key of an `updateRecord` or `createRecord` `set` names a computed field, a sequence field, a field with `match` or a child collection, which a process cannot set. A `createRecord` `set` cannot name a creator field either, as the server sets it to `system`. Reported at `/steps/{i}/set/{field}`. |
| `AXC0081` | A process task step's `assignee` has both or neither of `user` and `role`. Reported at `/steps/{i}/assignee`, or at `/steps/{i}/escalations/{j}/reassign` for an escalation's `reassign`. |
| `AXC0082` | A process task step's `form` names no loaded form, ignoring letter case. Reported at `/steps/{i}/form`. Not reported when the name is a form file that was not loaded because of its own errors. |
| `AXC0083` | A process task step's `form` is over another entity than the process's subject entity. Reported at `/steps/{i}/form`, naming both entities. Not checked when either entity is unknown. |
| `AXC0084` | A process task step's `dueIn`, its `deadline`'s `after`, an escalation's `after`, a timer step's `wait`, or a `waitEvent` step's `timeout.after`, is not a positive ISO 8601 duration in the accepted form: whole-number weeks (`W`), or days (`D`) with an optional `T` part of hours (`H`), minutes (`M`) and seconds (`S`), such as `P2W`, `P3D`, `PT4H` or `P1DT12H`. Years and months have no fixed length, so they are `AXC0084`. Reported at `/steps/{i}/dueIn`, at `/steps/{i}/deadline/after` for a deadline, at `/steps/{i}/escalations/{j}/after` for an escalation, at `/steps/{i}/wait` for a timer, or at `/steps/{i}/timeout/after` for a `waitEvent`. |
| `AXC0085` | An earlier outcome of the same task step already uses this `name`, ignoring letter case. Reported at `/steps/{i}/outcomes/{j}/name` of the later outcome, naming the first one. |
| `AXC0089` | A process task step's `assignee.role` names no declared role, compared exactly. Reported at `/steps/{i}/assignee/role`, or at `/steps/{i}/escalations/{j}/reassign/role` for an escalation's `reassign`. Not reported when the name is a role file that was not loaded because of its own errors. |
| `AXC0096` | An earlier operation of the same connector already uses this `name`, or an earlier field of the same operation's `input` or `output` already uses this field `name`, ignoring letter case. Reported at `/operations/{i}/name`, `/operations/{i}/input/{j}/name`, `/operations/{i}/output/{j}/name` or, for a nested field of an `object` or `list` input, `/operations/{i}/input/{j}/fields/{k}/name` of the later one. |
| `AXC0097` | A connector operation's `path` has an unbalanced `{` or `}`, or a placeholder that is not `{idempotencyKey}` and names no `path` input of the operation, such as `{orderId}`. Names match ignoring letter case. Reported at `/operations/{i}/path`. |
| `AXC0098` | A connector operation's `lookup` names no operation of the connector, ignoring letter case, or names its own operation. Or the operation it names is not a `GET`, has no `{idempotencyKey}` in its `path`, has inputs, or has other outputs than the operation it serves, including a different `source`. The message says which. Reported at `/operations/{i}/lookup`. |
| `AXC0099` | A connector's `timeout` is not a positive ISO 8601 duration of at most `PT2M`, in the form a task's `dueIn` takes, such as `PT30S`. Reported at `/timeout`. |
| `AXC0100` | The tenant a release is activated for lacks a binding a connector names, or holds it with another kind. `baseUrl` needs a `url` binding, and `auth` needs a `secret` binding. An `oauth2` auth's `tokenUrl` needs a `url` binding and its `clientId` needs a `secret` binding, like its `clientSecret`. The same applies to the bindings of an identity provider: an `oidc` provider's `issuer` needs a `url` binding and its client bindings need `secret` bindings, and a `saml` provider's `metadata` needs a `url` binding. Reported by activation at `/baseUrl/binding`, `/auth/token/binding`, `/auth/value/binding`, `/auth/tokenUrl/binding`, `/auth/clientId/binding` or `/auth/clientSecret/binding` of the connector file, or at `/issuer/binding`, `/clientId/binding`, `/clientSecret/binding`, `/clientKey/binding` or `/metadata/binding` of the identity provider file. The same applies to the `capabilities` of a package operation that a process step uses: `urls` need `url` bindings and `secrets` need `secret` bindings, reported at `/steps/{i}/operation` of the process file. The message names the tenant and the binding, never its value. Nothing is provisioned or activated. |
| `AXC0101` | Outside Development, a connector's `baseUrl` binding or the `tokenUrl` binding of its `oauth2` auth is not an `https` URL. Reported by activation at `/baseUrl/binding` or `/auth/tokenUrl/binding` of the connector file. The same applies to a `url` binding in the `capabilities` of a package operation that a process step uses, reported at `/steps/{i}/operation` of the process file. The message names the tenant and the binding, never its value. Nothing is provisioned or activated. |
| `AXC0102` | A process `callConnector` step's `connector` `name` names no loaded connector, ignoring letter case. Reported at `/steps/{i}/connector/name`. Not reported when the name is a connector file that was not loaded because of its own errors. Its `input` and `output` are then not checked. In a module process the name is a connector slot of the module, matched exactly, so an application connector's name is reported in the module file. |
| `AXC0103` | A process `callConnector` step's `connector` `operation` names no operation of its connector, ignoring letter case. Reported at `/steps/{i}/connector/operation`. Its `input` and `output` are then not checked. In a module process the operation is one of the connector slot, and the code is reported in the module file. |
| `AXC0104` | A key of a `callConnector` step's `input` names no input of the operation, or an input an earlier key of the step already names, ignoring letter case. Reported at `/steps/{i}/input/{key}`. |
| `AXC0105` | A required input of the operation a `callConnector` step calls has no key in the step's `input`. Reported at `/steps/{i}/input`, once for each missing input. |
| `AXC0106` | A key of a `callConnector` step's `output` names no output of the operation, or an output an earlier key of the step already names, ignoring letter case. Reported at `/steps/{i}/output/{key}`. |
| `AXC0107` | A value of a `callConnector` step's `output` names no field of the subject entity, or a field an earlier output of the step already writes, ignoring letter case. Reported at `/steps/{i}/output/{key}`. |
| `AXC0108` | A value of a `callConnector` step's `output` names a computed field, a sequence field, a field with `match` or a child collection, or a field whose type does not fit the output's. An output fits a field of the same type, and an `integer` output also fits a `decimal` field. Reported at `/steps/{i}/output/{key}`. |
| `AXC0109` | A `callConnector` step's retry `backoff` is not a positive ISO 8601 duration in the form `dueIn` accepts, such as `PT30S` or `P1D`. Reported at `/steps/{i}/retry/backoff`. |
| `AXC0110` | An event's `entity` names no loaded entity. Reported at `/entity`. Not reported when the name is an entity file that was not loaded because of its own errors. |
| `AXC0111` | An event's `entity` is a child entity. Reported at `/entity`, naming the owner. |
| `AXC0112` | An earlier field of the same event already uses this field `name`, ignoring letter case. Reported at `/fields/{i}/name` of the later one, naming the first. |
| `AXC0113` | An event field's `type` is `enum`, `reference` or `child-collection`. An event field is `text`, `integer`, `decimal`, `boolean`, `date` or `date-time`. Reported at `/fields/{i}/type`. |
| `AXC0114` | An endpoint's `entity` names no loaded entity. Reported at `/entity`. Not reported when the name is an entity file that was not loaded because of its own errors. |
| `AXC0115` | An endpoint's `entity` is a child entity. Reported at `/entity`, naming the owner. |
| `AXC0116` | An endpoint's `process` names no loaded process. Reported at `/process`. Not reported when the name is a process file that was not loaded because of its own errors. |
| `AXC0117` | An endpoint's `process` is over another entity than the endpoint's `entity`, or has no subject. Reported at `/process`, naming both entities, or saying the process has no subject. |
| `AXC0118` | An earlier input field of the same endpoint already uses this `name`, ignoring letter case. Reported at `/input/{i}/name` of the later one, naming the first. |
| `AXC0119` | A `child-collection` input field's `target` is not a child entity. Reported at `/input/{i}/target`. |
| `AXC0120` | An endpoint's `values` key names no field of the entity, or a field that an earlier key already maps, ignoring letter case. Reported at `/values/{key}`. |
| `AXC0121` | An endpoint's `values` key names a computed field, or a field with a `sequence`, `creator` or `match`, which the server sets, or a `references` field, which an endpoint cannot map yet. Reported at `/values/{key}`. |
| `AXC0122` | An endpoint maps a `child-collection` field to something other than the name of a `child-collection` input with the same target. Reported at `/values/{key}`. |
| `AXC0123` | An endpoint's `output` entry names no field of the entity, ignoring letter case. Reported at `/output/{i}`. |
| `AXC0124` | An endpoint's `wait` is not a valid ISO 8601 duration, is zero, or is above `PT30S`. Reported at `/wait`. |
| `AXC0125` | A `raiseEvent` or `waitEvent` step's `event`, or an endpoint's `event`, names no loaded event, ignoring letter case. Reported at `/steps/{i}/event`, or `/event/name` for an endpoint. Not reported when the name is an event file that was not loaded because of its own errors. |
| `AXC0126` | A `raiseEvent` step's `event` is about another entity than the process's, or an endpoint's `event` about another entity than the endpoint's. Reported at `/steps/{i}/event`, or `/event/name` for an endpoint, naming both entities. Not checked when either entity is unknown. |
| `AXC0127` | A key of a `raiseEvent` step's `fields`, or of an endpoint event's `fields`, names no payload field of the event, or a field an earlier key already names, ignoring letter case. Reported at `/steps/{i}/fields/{key}`, or `/event/fields/{key}` for an endpoint. |
| `AXC0128` | A payload field of the event that no key of a `raiseEvent` step's `fields`, or of an endpoint event's `fields`, names. Reported at `/steps/{i}/fields`, or `/event/fields` for an endpoint, once for each missing field. |
| `AXC0129` | A trigger's `process` names no loaded process, ignoring letter case. Reported at `/process`. Not reported when the name is a process file that was not loaded because of its own errors. |
| `AXC0130` | A trigger's `event` names no loaded event, ignoring letter case. Reported at `/event`. Not reported when the name is an event file that was not loaded because of its own errors. |
| `AXC0131` | A trigger's `event` is about another entity than the process's subject entity. Reported at `/event`, naming both entities. Not checked when either entity is unknown. |
| `AXC0143` | A data change trigger's `entity` names no loaded entity. Reported in the trigger at `/entity`. |
| `AXC0144` | A data change trigger's `entity` is not the process's subject entity. Reported in the trigger at `/entity`. |
| `AXC0145` | A name in a data change trigger's `fields` names no field of the entity, or names a computed field. Reported in the trigger at `/fields/{i}`. |
| `AXC0160` | A schedule trigger's `schedule` is not a valid 5-field cron. Reported at `/schedule`. |
| `AXC0161` | A schedule trigger's `timeZone` is not an IANA time zone id known to the machine, or is written in another letter case. Reported at `/timeZone`. |
| `AXC0162` | A schedule trigger's `dataSource` names no loaded data source, matched ignoring letter case. Reported at `/dataSource`. |
| `AXC0163` | A schedule trigger's `dataSource` names a grouped data source. Reported at `/dataSource`. |
| `AXC0164` | A schedule trigger's `dataSource` is over another entity than the process's subject entity. Reported at `/dataSource`. |
| `AXC0165` | A key in a schedule trigger's `parameters` names no parameter of the data source, or repeats a parameter that an earlier key gives, ignoring letter case. Without a data source, the keys are checked against the parameters of the process. A delete trigger's keys are checked against the parameters of its process. Reported at `/parameters/{key}`. |
| `AXC0166` | A value in a schedule trigger's `parameters` is not a literal of its parameter's type, or is `null` for a required parameter. Without a data source, `null` is never a literal of a process parameter. A value in a delete trigger's `parameters` that is not a string is this code too. Reported at `/parameters/{key}`. |
| `AXC0167` | A schedule trigger does not give a parameter of its data source, or of its process when it has no data source. A delete trigger does not give a parameter of its process. Reported at `/parameters`, once for each missing parameter. |
| `AXC0244` | A process has both `entity` and `parameters`, or neither. Reported at `/parameters` when it has both, and at the root of the file, with the path `""`, when it has neither. A process with both is then checked as a process with a subject. A process with neither is checked for its step graph and text keys only. |
| `AXC0245` | An earlier parameter of the same process already uses this `name`, ignoring letter case. Reported at `/parameters/{i}/name` of the later parameter, naming the first one. See [Processes without a subject](process-flow.md#processes-without-a-subject). |
| `AXC0246` | A step of a process without a subject needs a subject record: a `task` or `waitEvent` step, reported at `/steps/{i}/type`, an `updateRecord` or `raiseEvent` step, reported at `/steps/{i}/operation`, or a non-empty `output` on a `callConnector` or package operation step, reported at `/steps/{i}/output`. The other properties of such a step are not checked. |
| `AXC0247` | A trigger names a process without a subject. Reported at `/process`, for an event, a data change and a schedule trigger with a data source. It does not apply to a delete trigger, which must name such a process. |
| `AXC0248` | A `sum`, `avg`, `min` or `max` measure reads a `money` field with a `currencyField`, and the data source has no group field that reaches that currency field on the same record, through the same reference fields. Without it a total could add two currencies. A `count`, a `countDistinct` and a money field with a fixed `currency` need no group. Reported at `/aggregate/measures/{i}/field`. |
| `AXC0249` | A schedule trigger without a `dataSource` names a process with a subject. A scheduled job starts a process without a subject. Reported at `/process`. |
| `AXC0250` | The `process` of a `subProcess` step names no process of the application. Reported at `/steps/{i}/process`. A process whose file was not loaded because of its own errors is not reported again. See [Sub-process step](process-flow.md#sub-process-step). |
| `AXC0251` | A process reaches itself through `subProcess` steps, directly or through other processes, so the instances would start each other for ever. Reported once for each step that closes the loop, at `/steps/{i}/process`, naming the chain, such as `'A' → 'B' → 'A'`. |
| `AXC0252` | A `subProcess` step gives no `subject`, but the process it starts has a subject entity other than the parent's, or the parent has no subject. Reported at `/steps/{i}`. A `subject` given for a process without a subject is reported at `/steps/{i}/subject`. |
| `AXC0253` | The `parameters` of a `subProcess` step do not fit the process it starts. `parameters` given for a process with a subject are reported at `/steps/{i}/parameters`, and so is a parameter of the child that the step does not give. A key that is not a parameter of the child, or that repeats one, ignoring letter case, is reported at `/steps/{i}/parameters/{name}`. |
| `AXC0254` | A data change trigger lists `delete` with `create` or `update` in `on`. A delete trigger lists `delete` alone. Reported at `/on`. |
| `AXC0255` | A delete trigger has `fields`. A delete removes the whole record, so no field changes. Reported at `/fields`. |
| `AXC0256` | A delete trigger names a process with a subject. The record is gone, so a delete trigger starts a process without a subject. Reported at `/process`. |
| `AXC0260` | The `entity` of a `createRecord` step names no loaded entity. Reported at `/steps/{i}/entity`. Not reported when the name is an entity file that was not loaded because of its own errors. See [Create record operation](process-flow.md#create-record-operation). |
| `AXC0261` | The `entity` of a `createRecord` step is a child entity. A step creates a record of a root entity. Reported at `/steps/{i}/entity`, naming the owner. |
| `AXC0266` | A `notify` step has an empty `to`. Reported at `/steps/{i}/to`. |
| `AXC0267` | A recipient of a `notify` step has none or more than one of `user`, `role` and `email`. Reported at `/steps/{i}/to/{j}`. |
| `AXC0268` | A `role` recipient of a `notify` step names no declared role, compared exactly. A role file that was not loaded is not reported again. Reported at `/steps/{i}/to/{j}/role`. |
| `AXC0269` | A `notify` step has an empty `channels`. Reported at `/steps/{i}/channels`. |
| `AXC0270` | A `role` recipient of a `notify` step is used with the `email` channel. A role has no email address. Reported at `/steps/{i}/to/{j}/role`. |
| `AXC0271` | A placeholder of a `notify` step's subject or body has no key in `values`, in a locale that has both texts. Reported at `/steps/{i}/message`, naming the locale and the placeholder. |
| `AXC0272` | A key of a `notify` step's `values` has no placeholder in the subject or the body, in a locale that has both texts. Reported at `/steps/{i}/message`, naming the locale and the key. |
| `AXC0273` | A text of a `notify` step holds a lone or invalid brace. A placeholder is `{name}` with ASCII letters and digits, and `{{` and `}}` write a brace. Reported at `/steps/{i}/message`, naming the locale, the text key and the offset. |
| `AXC0278` | A `timer` step has both `wait` and `until`, or neither. Reported at `/steps/{i}`. See [Timer step](process-flow.md#timer-step). |
| `AXC0280` | A task step's escalation has neither `reassign` nor `notify`. Reported at `/steps/{i}/escalations/{j}`. An escalation's `reassign` and `notify` report the codes of a task's `assignee` and of a `notify` step, such as `AXC0081`, `AXC0089`, `AXC0307` and `AXC0266` to `AXC0273`, under `/steps/{i}/escalations/{j}/reassign` and `/steps/{i}/escalations/{j}/notify`. |
| `AXC0281` | A task step's escalation `after` is equal to or longer than the step's `deadline.after`. An escalation must come before the deadline. Reported at `/steps/{i}/escalations/{j}/after`. Not reported when the step has no deadline, or when either duration is `AXC0084`. |
| `AXC0282` | A `values` expression of a `notify` step reads a field marked `sensitive`, directly, through a reference path, inside an aggregate or as an argument to a rule. Reported at `/steps/{i}/message/values/{key}`, naming the field. See [Placeholders](notifications.md#placeholders). |
| `AXC0283` | A `waitEvent` step's `event` is about another entity than the process's subject entity. Reported at `/steps/{i}/event`, naming both entities. Not reported when either entity is unknown, or in a process without a subject, where the step is `AXC0246`. See [Wait for event step](process-flow.md#wait-for-event-step). |
| `AXC0310` | A binding of a connector's `oauth2` auth has a name that the application also uses as another binding kind, in another connector's `baseUrl` or `auth`, the manifest's `fileStorage` or an identity provider, compared ignoring letter case. `tokenUrl` is a `url` binding, and `clientId` and `clientSecret` are `secret` bindings. Reported at `/auth/tokenUrl/binding`, `/auth/clientId/binding` or `/auth/clientSecret/binding` of the connector file, naming the other kind and the file that uses it. An identity provider that uses the same name reports `AXC0285` at its own binding. See [OAuth2 auth](integration-resources.md#oauth2-auth). |
| `AXC0311` | A connector output's `source` is not a valid JSON Pointer: it does not start with `/`, or a `~` is not followed by `0` or `1`. Reported at `/operations/{i}/output/{j}/source`. See [structured payloads](integration-resources.md#structured-payloads). |
| `AXC0312` | A connector operation input is placed where it cannot go. A `path` input has no `{name}` placeholder in the operation's `path`, ignoring letter case, is not `required` or is named `idempotencyKey`. Or a `body` input is on a `GET`. Or an `object` or `list` input has an `in` other than `body`, because only scalar inputs go outside the body. The message says which. Reported at `/operations/{i}/input/{j}`. See [structured payloads](integration-resources.md#structured-payloads). |
| `AXC0313` | The `event` of a `webhook` names no loaded event, or a module event that is not found. A name under a module that the application does not declare is `AXC0207`. Reported at `/event`. See [webhook resource](outbound-webhooks.md#webhook-resource). |
| `AXC0314` | A `webhook` has no `url` or no `secret` binding. Reported at `/url` or `/secret`, once for each missing one. See [webhook resource](outbound-webhooks.md#webhook-resource). |
| `AXC0315` | The `timeout` of a `webhook` is not a positive ISO 8601 duration of at most `PT30S`. Reported at `/timeout`. See [webhook resource](outbound-webhooks.md#webhook-resource). |
| `AXC0316` | A `fields` expression of a `raiseEvent` step reads a field marked `sensitive`, directly, through a reference path, inside an aggregate or as an argument to a rule, and a `webhook` names the step's event. Reported at `/steps/{i}/fields/{key}`, naming the field and the event. The same code applies to an endpoint event's `fields` expression that reads an input that `values` maps to a `sensitive` field, reported at `/event/fields/{key}`. See [webhook resource](outbound-webhooks.md#webhook-resource). |
| `AXC0317` | A connector operation input nests an `object` or `list` too deep: an `object` or `list` is among the `fields` of one at level 4, counting the input as level 1. Reported at the path of the too deep field, such as `/operations/{i}/input/{j}/fields/{k}/fields/{l}/fields/{m}/fields/{n}`. The fields below it are not checked again. See [structured payloads](integration-resources.md#structured-payloads). |
| `AXC0318` | A `callConnector` step maps an input in a shape that does not fit it: a plain expression for an `object` or `list`, `fields` for a scalar input, a `from` on an `object`, or a `list` without `from`. Reported at the path of the mapping, such as `/steps/{i}/input/{key}` or `/steps/{i}/input/{key}/fields/{name}`. See [structured payloads](integration-resources.md#structured-payloads). |
| `AXC0319` | The `from` of a `list` mapping in a `callConnector` step is not a child collection of the subject, or the process has no subject. The item names and required fields are still checked, but not the item expressions. Reported at `/steps/{i}/input/{key}/from`. See [step shape](connector-and-package-calls.md#step-shape). |
| `AXC0359` | A branch of a decision step, or an escalation of a task step, has the `name` of an earlier one in the same list, ignoring letter case. Reported at `/steps/{i}/branches/{j}/name` or `/steps/{i}/escalations/{j}/name` of the later element. A missing `name` is `AXC0004`. See [steps](processes.md#steps), [escalations](task-deadlines.md#escalations-and-deadline) and [list keys](customization-layers.md#list-keys). |
| `AXC0363` | A schedule trigger's `dataSource` names a data source whose `filter` calls `currentUser()`. A schedule starts the process with no signed-in user. Reported at `/dataSource`. See [schedule triggers](integration-resources.md). |
| `AXC0378` | _(planned for M38)_ A transition crosses a block boundary. A step inside a branch or a body names a step outside its list, or a step outside a block names a step inside it. Reported at the transition. See [structure](process-blocks.md#structure). |
| `AXC0379` | _(planned for M38)_ Blocks nest deeper than the limit. Reported at the block that is too deep. See [structure](process-blocks.md#structure) and [limits](process-blocks.md#limits). |
| `AXC0380` | _(planned for M38)_ A `parallel` step has no branches, a branch has no `name`, `start` or `steps`, or a branch repeats the `name` of an earlier one, ignoring letter case. Reported at `/steps/{i}/branches` or `/steps/{i}/branches/{j}`. See [parallel step](process-blocks.md#parallel-step). |
